# Selected reading

Technical books I am working through alongside projects (not a full reading log).

- **Zero to Production in Rust**, Luca Palmieri
  Production minded Rust backend path; paired with [zero2prod](https://github.com/aquinoonell/zero2prod).
- **The Rust Programming Language** (*The Rust Book*), Steve Klabnik & Carol Nichols
  Core language foundations; reinforced with Rustlings and day to day Rust work.
- **Programming Rust**, Jim Blandy, Jason Orendorff & Leonora F. S. Tindall
  Deeper ownership, lifetimes, traits, and concurrency after the official book.
- **Rust for Rustaceans**, Jon Gjengset
  Advanced patterns for reading and writing production Rust.
- **Designing Data Intensive Applications**, Martin Kleppmann
  How modern data systems are designed: storage, replication, partitioning, and stream processing.
- **Ray Tracing in One Weekend**, Peter Shirley
  From scratch renderer math and materials; implemented in [Ray Tracer](https://github.com/aquinoonell/Ray-Tracer).
- **The Ray Tracing Road to Rust**
  Idiomatic Rust companion to Shirley's book while structuring the tracer.
